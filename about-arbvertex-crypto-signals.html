What is crypto futures arbitrage?
Crypto futures arbitrage is the practice of profiting from price differences of the same asset across different exchanges or between spot and futures markets. You buy on the cheaper exchange and simultaneously sell on the more expensive one, locking in profit when the gap closes. It is market-neutral — you don't need to predict price direction.
What is funding rate arbitrage?
Funding rate arbitrage involves holding a long spot position and shorting the perpetual futures when the funding rate is positive. You collect the 8-hourly funding fee as passive income while your combined position stays delta-neutral — unaffected by price movement. We signal both cross-exchange and funding rate opportunities.
