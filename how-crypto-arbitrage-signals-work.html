📡How We Monitor Markets

ArbVertex monitors live price feeds and funding rates across 7 major exchanges simultaneously — Binance, Bybit, MEXC, Gate.io, KuCoin, Bitget, and OKX. Every USDT trading pair is tracked in real time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The system compares prices across all exchange pairs every few seconds. When a gap appears between the price of the same coin on two different exchanges — or when a funding rate crosses a profitable threshold — the detection engine flags it for validation.

Over 700+ USDT pairs are monitored simultaneously. Most gaps are too small, too short-lived, or too risky to signal. Only opportunities that pass all validation checks reach your Telegram.

🔍Signal Detection Process

Two types of opportunities are detected and checked before a signal is sent:

01

Price Gap Detection

When the same coin is priced differently on two exchanges, the system calculates the gross gap percentage. A gap of 0.5% is common — but after fees, it yields near zero. The system looks for gaps of 2%+ to ensure net profit after all costs.

02

Funding Rate Opportunity

When a perpetual futures funding rate exceeds 0.03% per 8 hours and has been stable for multiple consecutive periods, the system flags it as a cash-and-carry opportunity. The funding rate is cross-checked across all exchanges to identify the best short leg placement.

03

Fee Calculation

The system calculates exact fees for both legs — maker/taker fees on spot and futures, withdrawal fees where applicable, and estimated slippage based on order book depth. Only opportunities with net profit above 1% after all fees proceed to validation.

✅Signal Validation

Most detected opportunities are filtered out at this stage. Validation checks confirm the opportunity is real, executable, and safe before a signal is sent.

01

Liquidity Check

The order book depth on both exchanges is checked. If the available liquidity at the target price is insufficient to fill a standard position without moving the price, the signal is discarded. Low liquidity = high slippage = false opportunity.

02

Persistence Check

The gap must persist for a minimum threshold before signalling. A gap that appears for 2 seconds and closes is not actionable for manual traders. Signals are only sent when the gap has been stable for at least 15–30 seconds.

03

Risk Assessment

Each signal is assigned a risk level based on funding rate stability, exchange liquidity depth, coin volatility, and withdrawal reliability. Risk levels — Low, Medium, High — are included in every signal so you can size positions accordingly.

04

Duplicate Filter

If a signal for the same pair and exchange combination was sent recently, it is suppressed to prevent alert fatigue. Each unique opportunity is signalled only once per window.

⚡Signal Types

ArbVertex delivers four types of signals — each suited to different capital levels and execution styles:

🟢 Funding Rate Arb

Long spot + short perpetual futures. Collect funding payments every 8 hours. Zero price exposure. Best for passive income — hold for days or weeks.

🔵 Cross-Exchange Spot

Buy on cheaper exchange, sell on more expensive one. Requires fast execution. Best for BTC, ETH, SOL with high liquidity and 2%+ gap.

🟠 Basis Trade

Profit from the spread between spot and quarterly futures at expiry. Longer hold (weeks to months). More predictable but lower annualised return.

🟣 Negative Funding

When funding turns negative — shorts pay longs. Reverse position (short spot + long perp) to collect the fee. Advanced strategy for experienced traders.

Risk levels across all signals:

🟢 LOW — Stable funding, high liquidity pairs 🟡 MEDIUM — Moderate gap, standard liquidity 🔴 HIGH — Large gap but lower liquidity or volatile pair

🎯How to Act on a Signal

Speed matters for spot arbitrage signals. Funding rate signals are less time-sensitive — the opportunity persists for hours or days. Here is the execution flow for both:

01

Check signal age

If the signal is older than 2 minutes for a spot arb, verify the gap still exists before executing. Open both exchange order books and confirm the price difference is still present.

02

Confirm your position size

Use the ArbVertex Calculator to confirm net profit after fees at your capital size. Never execute without knowing the exact net figure.

03

Execute both legs simultaneously

Open both exchange tabs in advance. Execute the buy and sell as close together as possible — ideally within 10–20 seconds. For funding rate arb, open spot buy first, then futures short.

04

Monitor and exit

For spot arb — close both legs as soon as fills are confirmed. For funding rate arb — monitor the funding rate before each 8-hour settlement. Exit if rate drops below +0.01%.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many signals are sent per day?
It depends on market conditions. In high-volatility periods, 5–15 signals per day are common. In quiet markets, 2–5 signals. We only send signals that pass all validation checks — quality over quantity.
How fast do I need to act on a signal?
For cross-exchange spot signals — within 1–2 minutes ideally. For funding rate signals — no urgency, the opportunity persists for hours or days. Signal age is always shown so you can judge immediately.
Do I need to be online all day?
No. Funding rate signals require no immediate action — you can check once per day before the 08:00 UTC settlement window. Spot arb signals require faster action, but you choose which signals to act on.
What exchanges do I need accounts on?
Binance and Bybit cover the majority of signals. Having accounts on Gate.io and OKX as well gives you access to nearly all signals. KuCoin, MEXC, and Bitget are optional — they appear less frequently but occasionally offer the best rates.
Are the net profit figures guaranteed?
No — net profit estimates are based on standard taker fees and estimated slippage at the time of detection. Actual results may vary slightly depending on your fee tier, execution speed, and market movement during execution. Always treat estimates as indicative.
What is the minimum capital to use the signals?
$200–$500 is a good starting point. Below $200, fees become a proportionally large share of profits. Most signals are sized for $500–$5,000 positions, though they scale to any amount.
